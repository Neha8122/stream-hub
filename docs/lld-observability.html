3 · SLOs: what "healthy" means, in numbers

Two SLOs for the home page

SLOGood eventTarget
Fast/home answered 2xx in under 300 ms99% over 30 days
Completea page with no degraded rows99% over 30 days

The second one matters because of step 3: a page that quietly serves stale rows still returns 200 in 20 ms. Without it, "history is down" would look perfectly healthy.

Error budget and burn rate

Error budget99% target → 1% of requests may be bad: ~7 hours of total outage in 30 days
Burn ratehow fast it's being used: 1× uses it in exactly 30 days, 14.4× in about 2 days
Page nowburn > 14.4× over both 1 h and 5 min: 2% of the month's budget gone in an hour
Ticketburn > 6× over both 6 h and 30 min: a slow leak

Two windows per alert: the long one proves it's real, the short one proves it's still happening, so an alert stops soon after the problem does.
